# Something they already have

A person who already sketched, mocked or prototyped what they want is built
toward it. Where they have something, it settles the question and no throwaway
is built.

## What counts

A mock or design, a clickable prototype, a screenshot of another product
meaning "like this", a hand sketch or whiteboard photo, a written brief, the
spreadsheet that is today's manual tool, a sample data file, an example of the
output wanted, a reference link, or a recording of the flow. A file in the
recorded design tool may be opened directly where the harness can reach it.

## Say back what you see

Read it, then say what you found with your guesses attached: "I see a list
with add, edit, and a search box, and deleting has no confirm step. Is that
right?" Where the harness cannot open the image or link, say so and ask for a
description or a screenshot. Never pretend to have seen it.

## What it settles, and what it never does

It settles look and behaviour, never the build path. A mock showing outside
sign-in, payments or stored personal information goes through the fit check
and earns the risk notice exactly as the same request in words would.

An artifact is a design, not production code. A coded prototype stays
throwaway: pinned down by tests or rebuilt. Genuinely working code is the
`setup-ai-build-kit` skill's `references/adopting.md`.

Never take real data or a secret out of an artifact. Real records in a
screenshot show the shape of the data and never become fixtures, and a password
visible in a picture is neither stored nor repeated.

## Cut it down

A mock usually shows more than one piece. Cut it into pieces at founding, or
build only the piece in hand. Name what it does not cover (missing screens,
empty states, errors, who may do what) and ask or park those. Where two
artifacts disagree, or one contradicts the masterplan, let the person choose.
Turn a loose reference such as "make it feel like this" into behaviours anybody
could check, and park what cannot be.

## Record the decision

Record the question, what was decided, and what the production build must
preserve, in words: in the masterplan at founding, on the piece afterwards. A
link that breaks later must not take the decision with it.
