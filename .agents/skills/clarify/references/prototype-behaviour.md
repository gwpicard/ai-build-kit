# Prototype: does this behave the way I meant

The person drives it. They press things, watch what happens, and say where it
does not match how the work really goes.

## What to build

One file they open by double-clicking, needing nothing installed and nothing
running, so it can be sent to whoever knows the work best and driven on their
own machine. Do not reach for the real project's parts, which need setting up.

## What it has to do

Label everything in the words of their work. Show the current state after every
action, in plain sight. Offer a guided path through the awkward cases, named in
their words: the booking that clashes, the form left half finished, the person
without permission, the same action twice. Use invented data that looks like
theirs, enough for those cases to happen.

## What to bring back

The decision in one line, and what the production build must keep. A
correction about how the work really goes goes into the record.
