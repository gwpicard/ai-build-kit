# Prototype: what shape should this take

The person sees different arrangements of the same thing and says which is
right, or that they want the top of one and the bottom of another.

## How many

Three by default. More when asked, and say that past about five they stop
differing in ways anybody can judge.

## What has to differ

Layout and order: what sits at the top, what is grouped with what, what is one
screen or two, what is hidden until asked for. Never colour, wording or
spacing, which read as a choice that was never open.

## Where to show them

Inside the real page, with its real surroundings and data, wherever one exists.
An arrangement on an empty screen always looks fine. Give a way to move between
the versions that is in view where they are looking. That control may look like
what it is; the arrangements never announce that they are prototypes.

Where a design tool is recorded and the harness can reach it, its canvas can
hold the alternatives before a real page exists. Otherwise use a coded
throwaway. Where the harness has a browser tool, look at your own throwaway
first, in a browser on this computer, as the `setup-ai-build-kit` skill's
`references/capability-check.md` says. Say when you could not inspect it.

## What to bring back

Which arrangement was chosen and what the production build must keep. Where
they wanted parts of two, write down which parts.
