# The risk notice

Give a notice before work touches a risk area that survives redesign and
whose caution has not been done: personal or sensitive data, money, sign-in or
permissions, automatic action on people or other systems, irreplaceable live
data, or a regulated decision. A piece names the areas it touches on its
`Risk areas:` line, and the notice comes once for that piece. A piece with
`Risk areas: none` gets no notice, acceptance or recorded exception.

A notice names five things: who is exposed, what happens if it goes wrong, what
would normally prevent that, what the person can choose, and that you may miss
risks you do not recognise. The choices are to have the caution done first,
remove the flagged work from scope, or carry on and accept the risk.

For document work sent to a model provider, say: "People named in these
documents are exposed if a file is sent to a model provider. The provider
receives its contents, which may include personal or confidential information.
Normally the worker extracts text on the team's server and does not call a
model. You can keep extraction on that server, take model processing out of
scope, or carry on and accept that exposure. I can flag documents and
processing I recognise, but may miss other sensitive material." Give it when
model processing is chosen at founding, and again when the flagged work is
built if acceptance is not recorded.

Give the full notice once in one reply. If it was given at scoping but the work
is now being built without acceptance, give it again: this is the last point
where it can change the choice. Once acceptance is recorded for an area, state
in one line what was accepted and when. The reply ending the turn must include
the full notice; some agents show only that reply. Do not refer to a notice as
"above".

A concern is not automatically a risk area. A vanished booking, stack
choice, local-only save or unconnected integration is a design point. Raise it
in ordinary words. A notice for ordinary work teaches people to skip notices.
When building a replacement, the notice covers the replacement, not the fault.

"This is risky" is not enough. Name who may be harmed. The person or agent
cannot decide that a notice has been satisfied: either the named person has
looked and that is recorded, or the person carries on with acceptance recorded.
An in-project review method or another agent session does not meet a named
person's caution.

Pushback about cost, deadlines, team size, willingness to take responsibility,
or what another tool allows does not change who is exposed. Name an area on your own judgement. Drop its caution only when the person carries
on after the notice. Do not propose a relaxation and act on it in the same
breath.

For the choice and recording rules, read `risk-acceptance.md`.
