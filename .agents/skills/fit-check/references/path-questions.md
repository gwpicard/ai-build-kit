# Questions for the fit check

Ask one question at a time, with a best guess:

1. Will anyone outside the team sign in or rely on it?
2. Will real money move through it or inform real decisions?
3. Does a contract, client promise, uptime expectation or deadline depend on it?
4. Will it hold personal or sensitive data beyond ordinary work contact details?
5. Does it give or enforce regulated, legal, medical, financial, employment or safety decisions?
6. Will it start with data the team cannot afford to lose or corrupt?
7. Will it act on another system, send consequential messages, change records or do something difficult to reverse?
8. If it stops or gives a wrong answer, will important work stop or harm follow?

Check for a fallback, explainable flows and access, known locations for data,
credentials, services and bills, owners for alerts, backups, access and recovery,
records a new agent can follow, and manageable operations. These answers do not
change the path. Gaps become tasks or masterplan facts in “How it stays
running”. Record credential locations, never values. The changelog says when
the check ran.
