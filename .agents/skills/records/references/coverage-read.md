# Coverage read

Compare what the masterplan promises with what the pieces would build, name
what nothing builds, and change nothing by yourself. It runs at the end of
founding, once the pieces are cut, and in every /maintain truing.

## What to compare

The masterplan's promises: what it does and for whom, the main flow, what
correct looks like, what happens on failure, who can see and do what, the data
it holds and where it comes from, and what it connects to.

Every piece, open and closed. A closed piece counts as covered. Match by plain
description. A promise whose only piece is parked is a gap. Read each piece's
`## Masterplan change` beside its result: an unapplied change on a landed piece
belongs to the truing, and a future change on an open piece is not yet a
promise.

Compare the masterplan's key terms with the settled terms in every piece's
`## Decided` and `## Masterplan change`, parked and reshaped ones included. A
term missing from the masterplan, or meaning something different, is a record
gap.

## What to say

With no gap, one line: "Everything the masterplan promises has a piece that
builds it."

Otherwise one short list and one offer, terms included: "Everything the
masterplan promises has a piece that builds it, except two. Nothing builds the
weekly summary email, and nothing builds the rule that a job cannot be closed
twice. Shall I add those to the plan?"

A gap they want becomes a piece. A promise they decide against moves to the
masterplan's out-of-scope section. Where they say a promise is covered, they
are right. A missing term goes into the masterplan on a yes: during founding
directly, otherwise through the truing. A term for work still only planned
stays on its piece.

It never names a promise not on the page, never adds, edits, closes or
relabels a piece without a yes, never asks twice, and blocks nothing. On
Explore privately, report and stop without offering pieces.
