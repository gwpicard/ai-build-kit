# Document processing

Extract text on the team's own server by default. A model receives documents
only after the team opts in.

At founding, say: "The people named in these documents could be exposed if the
text is stored or used outside the company. The model provider receives the
documents to process them. Keep plain text extraction on your own server, or
choose to send documents to the model. I flag what I can recognise and may
miss other risks. Do you want to opt in to model processing?"

Record acceptance in the build-path record before sending a document. Without
that choice, keep extraction on the team's own server.
