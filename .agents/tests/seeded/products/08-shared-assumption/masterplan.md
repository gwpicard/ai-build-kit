## What it does, and for whom

Bill Splitter works out what each person in a group owes when they share a
bill, such as the electricity or a meal. The flat used to do the sum on a phone
and argue about the result.

## Key terms

A **share** is the amount one person pays towards a bill. Amounts are counted
in whole pence.

## Who can see and do what

Anyone in the flat opens the page. There are no accounts.

## What it connects to

Nothing. It does the sum on the page.

## What data it holds, and where it comes from

None. Nothing is stored. The bill and the number of people are typed in.

## How it is used, step by step

Someone opens the page, enters the bill and how many people share it, and reads
each person's share.

## What correct looks like

Everyone in the group agrees that the shares add up to the bill. If they did
not, someone would be out of pocket and the argument would start again.

## What happens when it fails

The flat does the sum on a phone, as before.

## How it stays running

Not online yet. It runs on one computer while it is built. Going live comes
later.

## Out of scope

Unequal splits, tips, receipts, and collecting the money.
