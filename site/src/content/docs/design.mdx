## Why the kit exists

People can use a coding agent to make a working tool quickly. A prototype can become hard to change when nobody knows where its data lives or what to do when it fails. AI Build Kit gives the owner a way to build and maintain a tool without reading code.

The kit suits internal tools for a team, club, family or invited clients. It does not suit someone who wants an app in minutes and does not want to use GitHub. A hosted builder may suit that need better. The kit does not teach programming or replace a developer when one is needed.

## Design choices

### Agree one piece at a time

You agree on a result that you can check before the agent builds it. A small task gives you a clear result to try. See the [shape step](/ai-build-kit/how-it-works/#shape-a-piece). This follows the rule that each capability must say what you see, when you see it, and what you do if it goes wrong ([philosophy](https://github.com/gwpicard/ai-build-kit/blob/stable/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md#what-earns-a-place)).

### Use checks for rules a machine can judge

Scripts and hooks check rules that a machine can decide. Instructions cover judgements that need a person or agent. This keeps each rule with the method that can check it ([philosophy](https://github.com/gwpicard/ai-build-kit/blob/stable/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md#what-earns-a-place)).

### Keep instructions short

Each instruction uses the agent's attention. Extra rules can make the important rules less likely to be followed. The kit keeps only rules that machines enforce, measurements support or a person must judge ([philosophy](https://github.com/gwpicard/ai-build-kit/blob/stable/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md#small-is-a-reliability-feature)).

### Warn once when you can judge the risk

The kit explains who may be exposed and what would normally prevent harm. You decide whether to address the risk or accept it. The kit records your words and the date. A judgement risk belongs to the piece that touches it ([risk design](https://github.com/gwpicard/ai-build-kit/blob/stable/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md#the-risk-concept)).

### Keep the tool in your accounts

The code, host and data use accounts that you control. You can change coding agent, host or developer later without losing ownership of the tool ([philosophy](https://github.com/gwpicard/ai-build-kit/blob/stable/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md#what-the-kit-is)).

### Test outcomes

A test checks what happened, not whether a sentence appears. Shell checks test boundaries and scripts. Replays grade what the agent did and what it left in the project ([testing](https://github.com/gwpicard/ai-build-kit/blob/stable/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md#testing-the-kit)). These results are evidence about the kit. They do not prove that every tool is safe for every purpose.
