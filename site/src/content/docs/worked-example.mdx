The team needs a log for shared camera and sound kit. Each step says what happened and what the person chose. The details show any checks listed in that run record. The terminals show separate recorded runs of version 1.0. They are not one continuous session.

Open a step to see the checks listed in that run record and its grade. A hit means the conversation shows the expected result. A miss means it shows a problem or leaves a result out. Drift means the agent reached the right result in a different way. Unobservable means the conversation cannot show what happened. Some runs include misses because we show the recorded results.
