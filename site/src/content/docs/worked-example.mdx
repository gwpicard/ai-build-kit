The example begins with a team planning a loan log for shared camera and sound equipment. Later steps draw on separate recorded test conversations, including work on a noticeboard, rather than one continuous project session. Each step summarises the conversation and the person’s decision; expand the details to see the listed checks. The conversation panels contain shortened excerpts.

Expand “Run details and checks” to see the listed checks and the grade. A hit means the conversation demonstrates the expected result; a miss means it shows a problem or omits an expected result. Drift means the agent reached the expected result by a different route, while unobservable means the conversation provides insufficient evidence to judge. The grades include misses as well as successes.
